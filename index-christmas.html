What We Do

Specialized Healthcare BPO Services

From the moment a claim is generated to the final dollar collected — we manage every step with precision.

Accounts Receivable Management
End-to-end AR lifecycle management that accelerates cash flow and minimizes aging receivables for US & Canadian healthcare organizations.
  • Invoice monitoring & lifecycle tracking
  • Customer payment follow-ups & collections
  • AR aging report preparation (30/60/90 days)
  • Receivable account reconciliation
  • Dispute tracking & coordination
  • Monthly receivable performance reporting
Record Review & Documentation Audit
Comprehensive documentation verification and audit to ensure compliance, completeness, and accuracy across all healthcare records.
  • Documentation verification & classification
  • Identification of missing or incomplete records
  • Data extraction & indexing
  • Chronological record summaries
  • Compliance verification vs. internal policies
Medical Transcription
Accurate transcription of clinical encounters, dictation, and patient documentation — delivered with speed and HIPAA-compliant security.
  • Physician dictation transcription
  • Clinical notes & discharge summaries

  • EMR-compatible structured output
  • 98%+ accuracy rate guaranteed
  • Turnaround within agreed SLA

Performance Metrics

KPIs We Track — Transparently

Every engagement is measured against proven industry benchmarks so you see real results, not just reports.

DSO
Days Sales Outstanding
Measures how quickly your organization collects payment after services are rendered. We target consistent reduction every quarter.
CEI
Collection Effectiveness Index
Tracks the percentage of collectable AR actually collected — a direct measure of collections efficiency.
30/60/90
Aging Receivable Reduction
Proactive management of AR buckets — reducing 60+ and 90+ day balances to improve working capital.
IAR
Invoice Accuracy Rate
Percentage of invoices processed without errors — minimizing rework, disputes, and payer denials.
RRA
Record Review Accuracy
Measures completeness and correctness of audited records — targeting 98%+ accuracy on every engagement.
RT
Reporting Timeliness
All reports delivered within agreed SLAs — weekly updates and a comprehensive monthly performance package.
